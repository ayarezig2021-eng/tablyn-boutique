Cookie and browser storage choices

Tablyn uses essential browser storage for basic site features and to remember your choices. The Studio can also save your design in your own browser when you press “Save design”. This design data stays in that browser unless you clear its storage.

Optional analytics and marketing storage should remain disabled unless the site owner adds those tools and obtains any required consent. The current preference controls below record your choice; they do not activate analytics or advertising services by themselves.
